Finding us

We are on Rajarampuri 6th Lane, in Yashodhara Apartment, office number 101 on the first floor. Rajarampuri is central and well served by city buses and autos — tell the driver "Rajarampuri sahavi gulli" and you will be dropped within a minute's walk.

Coming in from outside Kolhapur, the CBS bus stand and the railway station are both a ten to fifteen minute auto ride away. If you are driving down from Sangli or Belgaum, park on 6th Lane itself.

Planning to visit? Give us a call first on 77090 99599 so we can make sure a trainer is free to sit with you and show you what a class actually looks like.
