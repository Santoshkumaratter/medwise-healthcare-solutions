Our classroom is in Rajarampuri, five minutes from the middle of the city. If you are searching for medical coding classes in Kolhapur, this page tells you exactly what you would be joining.

Why medical coding, and why in Kolhapur

Kolhapur graduates a large number of pharmacy, nursing and life science students every year — from Shivaji University and its affiliated colleges, from Bharati Vidyapeeth, from D. Y. Patil, and from the pharmacy and science colleges spread across the district and out towards Warananagar and Ichalkaranji.

What the district does not have is a matching set of employers. Kolhapur's economy runs on foundries, textiles, sugar and jaggery. None of those need a B.Pharm graduate. So the pattern repeats every year: a good degree, a two-year gap, a medical representative job that involves more driving than science, and eventually a move to Pune with no particular plan.

Medical coding breaks that pattern without asking you to gamble. It is desk work in healthcare, it pays on your subject knowledge, and it is hired for in volume by companies with delivery centres a few hours away.

What our Kolhapur classroom actually looks like

Office No. 101, Yashodhara Apartment, on Rajarampuri 6th Lane. One room, twenty seats, a whiteboard that ends up covered in ICD-10 chapter ranges, and a trainer who codes charts for a living outside class hours.

Because we are central, most Kolhapur students walk, ride or take an auto and are here in fifteen minutes from anywhere between Rankala and the Shivaji University side. That matters more than it sounds — a class you can reach easily is a class you actually attend on the day you are tired.

  • Weekday evening — for candidates already working in a pharmacy, diagnostic lab or hospital

  • Live online — available to Kolhapur students too, if travelling in daily is a problem

Where Kolhapur students end up working

Our students have joined GeBBS Healthcare, IKS Health, Cotiviti, Omega Healthcare and Vee Healthtek, mostly at the Pune and Mumbai delivery centres, with some going to Bengaluru and Chennai. Pune is the common first posting for Kolhapur candidates — close enough to come home most weekends, big enough that a second job is easy to find once you have a year of production behind you.

A number of coders who started in an office have since moved to hybrid or fully remote roles and now work from Kolhapur permanently. That is increasingly common in this industry and it is worth knowing before you assume a coding career means leaving for good.
