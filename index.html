A healthcare career that does not need a hospital shift

Every treatment given anywhere in the United States has to be translated into a standard code before an insurance company will pay for it. That translation is medical coding, and it is done almost entirely from India.

Your degree is the entry ticket

B.Pharm, M.Pharm, B.Sc, BAMS, BHMS, BDS, BPT, Nursing, Microbiology, Biotechnology — the anatomy and pharmacology you already studied is exactly what this job runs on.

Day shifts, desk work, no patients

You read documentation on a screen and assign codes. No night duty, no clinical risk, no field work. Most employers now offer hybrid or work-from-home after training.

The demand is not slowing down

Omega, Vee Healthtek, IKS, GeBBS, AGS Health and Access Healthcare hire certified coders in bulk across Pune, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad every single quarter.

Straight talk about the salary. A fresher trainee coder in Maharashtra usually starts at ₹18,000–₹25,000 a month. Once you hold the CPC credential and clear a year or two of live production, ₹4–6 lakh a year is realistic, and senior coders and auditors go well past that. Anyone promising you ₹50,000 in month one is selling you something.

The books, tabbed properly

Mock exams under real conditions

Full-length timed papers before you sit for the certification, marked and reviewed question by question so you walk into the exam hall already knowing your pace.

Placement is part of the course

Resume writing, mock interviews, aptitude prep and direct referrals to our recruiter contacts. We keep working with you until you have an offer letter in hand.

Two certifications, both globally recognised

CPC is awarded by the AAPC and CCS by AHIMA — the two credentialing bodies every major healthcare BPO in India recruits against. We prepare you for the exam and the job that follows it.

AAPC credential · most in demand

CPC — Certified Professional Coder

The standard entry credential for outpatient and physician-side coding, and the one named in the majority of fresher job postings in India. Covers ICD-10-CM diagnosis coding, CPT procedure coding, HCPCS Level II, modifiers, E/M levelling and compliance.

Questions we get asked every week

Which is the best medical coding class in Kolhapur?

We will make the case for ourselves, and you should judge it against anyone else you are considering. MedWise Healthcare Solutions teaches medical coding and nothing else. Our faculty are certified coders with live production experience, batches are capped at twenty so nobody gets lost at the back, and placement support runs until you have an offer letter — not until the course ends.

The honest way to decide is to sit in a free demo class at all of them and compare. Ours is free and takes ninety minutes.

Can I join from Sangli, Satara or Belgaum?

Yes, and a good share of every batch does exactly that. Sangli and Ichalkaranji students usually take the weekend classroom batch in Kolhapur. Satara, Karad and Belgaum students more often take the live online batch, which is the same syllabus, the same trainer and the same mock exams, with recordings if you miss a session.

I have no computer background. Can I still do medical coding?

Yes. Medical coding is not software programming — the word "coding" here means assigning a standard code to a diagnosis or procedure. If you can use a browser and type, you have enough computer skill. What matters far more is your medical subject knowledge, which you already have from your degree.

What does the course cost, and can I pay in parts?

Fees depend on the certification you choose and whether you take the classroom or online batch. We publish the current figure over a call rather than on the website, because it changes with the exam voucher cost. Instalments are available, and we will tell you the total, including the AAPC or AHIMA exam fee, before you enrol — no charges appear later.

Is the 100% placement guarantee real?

It is, with one condition we state upfront: you have to complete the training, clear your certification and attend the interviews we arrange. We cannot place a student who stops attending. For everyone who finishes the programme, we keep referring and preparing you until you are placed.

Will I have to leave Kolhapur for the job?

Usually for the first role, yes. The large coding employers have their delivery centres in Pune, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad, and most freshers work from office for the initial training and production period. After that, a lot of coders move to hybrid or fully remote roles and come back home. Some employers now hire remote freshers directly, and we tell you when those openings come up.

How long does it take to become a certified medical coder?

About three months of training, then your certification attempt. Most students who join a regular weekday batch are certified and interviewing within four months of their first class. Weekend batches take around five to six months end to end.
